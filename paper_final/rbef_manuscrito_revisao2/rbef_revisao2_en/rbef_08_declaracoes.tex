\section*{Data availability}
The meteorological data used are public and were obtained from the Meteorological Database of the Brazilian National Institute of Meteorology~\cite{inmet2025}. The data file used and the notebook that reproduces all the figures and tables of this paper accompany the manuscript as supplementary material, together with a data sheet describing the origin of the data and the preparation steps. The results were obtained with Python 3.11, pyglam 1.1.0, NumPy 2.4, SciPy 1.17, pandas 2.3, and Matplotlib 3.11. With other versions, the unselected fitting attempts and the PNG files may differ slightly, without changing the tables at the published precision.
