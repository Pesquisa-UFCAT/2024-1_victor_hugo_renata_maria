# **METApy: BAYESIAN NETWORK FOR RELIABILITY ASSESSMENT OF RC STRUCTURES SUBJECTED TO CARBONATION-INDUCED CORROSION**
*Abstract: Reinforced concrete (RC) structures are widely used in civil construction due to their durability and economic efficiency. However, their long-term performance is affected by reinforcement corrosion, particularly when induced by carbonation. This dissertation proposes a methodology for assessing the reliability of RC structures subjected to carbonation-induced corrosion using Bayesian Networks (BNs). The developed model relies exclusively on theoretical data and numerical models to represent carbonation depth progression, material degradation, and structural performance over time. The approach employs predefined variables and probabilistic distributions to address the inherent uncertainties of the parameters, enabling the analysis of different exposure scenarios. The expected outcome is a robust and adaptable model that can serve as a basis for more practical and rapid reliability assessments, optimizing decision-making processes in the maintenance and management of structures.*
